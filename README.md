**Bayfront Heat 2** is an original top-down 2D open-world crime game built for the
make.fun `gta6-01` bounty, in plain HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript ES modules —
no engine, no build step, no network requests.

Walk or drive around the fictional city of Bayfront, split into four districts
(Downtown, The Docks, Palm Suburbs and Industrial Flats), each with its own look.
Jack any of five cars for simple arcade driving (acceleration, braking, turning,
a speed cap), dodge 24 autonomously wandering pedestrians, and watch your five-star
wanted level climb when you drive recklessly or hit someone — police cars spawn and
chase you until you lose the heat or get busted. Work through a four-job mission
chain for Freddy the fixer (meet him, steal a car, deliver it, then shake the cops),
tracked live in the HUD along with a minimap, health bar and cash. Progress saves to
and loads from `localStorage`, with a Continue option on the title screen.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts --port 8211
```

Then open http://localhost:8211 and click Start. WASD/arrows to move, E to
enter/exit a car, SHIFT to sprint on foot.
